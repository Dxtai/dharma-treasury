Dharma Treasury (佛法百宝箱) Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Dharma Treasury (佛法百宝箱, "the app") is made by 慈慈不息 The Benevolent. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We do not collect personal data

The app needs no account. It does not collect, upload or sell any personal data. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Data stored on your phone

For your convenience, the following is stored only on your own phone and is never sent to us or any third party:

  • Favourites, bookmarks and reading progress
  • Preferences such as text size, Simplified/Traditional Chinese and colour theme
  • Chanting counts, daily practice settings and practice records
  • Dedication verses you choose or write

Deleting the app deletes this data as well.

YouTube videos

Some Buddhist songs are played with the YouTube embedded player. When you play these videos, YouTube (Google) may process related data under its own policy; see the Google Privacy Policy. If you do not play YouTube videos, the app does not connect to YouTube.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
